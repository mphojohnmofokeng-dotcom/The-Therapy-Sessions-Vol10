After The Conversation

A New Age RnB & Soul Offering

Enter the session
01 / Welcome

You’ve had the conversation. Now sit with what it left behind.

After The Conversation is a 25 track session built around the records I felt belonged together.

I wanted this one to feel personal without over explaining itself. There are songs about love, growth, attraction, uncertainty, faith and simply taking a moment to breathe. The idea was to let the music carry the conversation.

I wasn't looking for a playlist. I was looking for a conversation.
02 / The idea

What is a New Age RnB & Soul Offering?

For me, it is not a strict genre. It is a way of approaching the music.

It is RnB and soul that can move between jazz, hip hop, electronic music, broken beats and other sounds without losing the feeling. It can sound familiar without trying to recreate the past. It can be modern without chasing whatever happens to be current.

Most importantly, the songs still have to make you feel something. That is the part that matters to me.

03 / The curation

25 tracks I felt belonged together.

Every track had to make sense beside the one before it and leave something for the one that follows. Some choices are obvious. Others are there because of a particular feeling, texture or transition.

That is what I enjoy about making these sessions. I am not only choosing songs. I am thinking about how one record changes the way you hear the next one.

04 / The records

The reasons behind the selection.

01DisclosureJill Scott+

I wanted to start with Jill because she immediately gives the session an emotional centre. There is intimacy in her voice, but there is also confidence. It felt like the right way to open the conversation.

02All Because of LoveCleo Sol+

This brings the warmth in early. I like how Cleo Sol can make something personal without making it feel forced. Love is the subject here, but the record still leaves room for the listener to bring their own experience to it.

03In the Name of LoveVictoria Noelle+

There is a softness to this one that I really wanted at this point. It keeps the mood intimate and lets the session move naturally without becoming too heavy too soon.

04In HeavenFKJ+

I needed some space here. FKJ gives the mix a different texture and allows the listener to settle into the production. Not every part of a conversation needs to be explained.

05Flowers in BloomTom Misch+

This one brings warmth and movement. I like the sense of change in it, especially because the session is eventually going to become more reflective.

06Laconic GestureArkose+

This is where I wanted the selection to become a little less obvious. I like records that reveal themselves slowly, and this gives the mix a different kind of depth.

07Discomfort ZoneNisa Faro+

The title alone made sense for the story I was building. Growth is not always comfortable, and I wanted the music to acknowledge that without making the session feel too serious.

08Play OnMelody Kin+

This gives us some movement again. After sitting with the previous record, I wanted something that keeps things moving without breaking the mood.

09Dreams / PrerollNisa Faro+

I like the space this record creates between the real and the imagined. It feels slightly unpredictable, which is useful here because the session is moving deeper into itself.

10HeavenMasego+

This brings some light back into the session. Masego has that ability to make a record feel relaxed and sensual without losing the musical detail.

11Let Me GrowThreetwenty+

This is one of the records that connects directly with the idea behind the session. Growing does not mean having everything figured out. Sometimes you just need the space to become.

12NguweOriiginelle+

I wanted to bring the conversation back to relationships and the things we carry into them. There is an honesty in this record that fits the point we are at in the mix.

13Don’t PlayJill Scott+

Bringing Jill back here changes the feeling from the opening track. There is more clarity now. I like the idea of hearing the same voice from a different place in the sequence.

14KryptoniteDelishia J+

We all have things that make us vulnerable. I wanted this record here because it adds that human side to the session. You can know yourself well and still have certain things that get to you.

15Living WaterVictoria Noelle+

This felt like the right place to slow down and reset. There is a spiritual quality to the record that gives the first half of the session a sense of release before we move toward connection again.

16FeedbackEkene+

I hear this as a conversation with yourself. That idea fits the project because sometimes the most useful feedback is the part you are willing to admit to yourself.

17MapulaMarcus Harvey+

This brings a warmth that I wanted as the session starts moving outward again. After spending time in reflection, it felt right to bring another sense of connection into the mix.

18No One But YouJustine Nozuka & Mahalia+

I really like the chemistry between Justine and Mahalia here. It feels natural, and that was important at this point in the sequence.

19EchosErykah Badu & The Alchemist+

This is one of those combinations that makes sense to me immediately. Erykah Badu and The Alchemist bring together soul, hip hop and a more experimental approach, which is very close to what I mean when I describe this as a New Age RnB and Soul offering.

20Apple JuiceRapsody+

I wanted some personality and playfulness here. Rapsody brings that balance while still giving the listener something to think about.

21EnergyTiana Major9 & Keyon Harrold+

At this point I wanted the energy to lift. The groove gives the session a different kind of momentum and makes the final stretch feel more open.

22Rather Be AloneBetty Brown+

I like the honesty in the title. After spending so much time thinking about connection, there is also something important about having your own space.

23CPRSummer Walker+

This brings the conversation back to what connection can do for us. It is direct, emotional and fits the way the final part of the session leans toward love again.

24Faith in SubstanceThreetwenty+

I wanted to finish the penultimate track with something grounded. You do not need to have every answer before you can keep moving forward.

25CrossfadeErykah Badu & The Alchemist+

I did not want the session to finish with a hard stop. Crossfade lets the whole thing ease out naturally. After the conversation, you are left with whatever the music brought up for you.

05 / The conversation

Maybe the music was the conversation all along.

I do not expect everyone to hear these records the way I hear them. That is part of the point. A good mix gives you enough of the story to enter it, then lets you make it your own.

If something in these 25 tracks stays with you after the session ends, then the music has done what I wanted it to do.
